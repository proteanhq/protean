Added a `protean eventstore dlq` command group to see the event-store dead-letter queue: `list` shows the positions a subscription exhausted (retried up to `max_retries` and gave up on), grouped by subscription, and `inspect <position>` re-reads the failing event. Both support `--json`. Exhausted records now also carry the failing event's `stream_name` and `stream_position` so `inspect` can locate the event; records written before this change fall back to the origin stream.
