The `reserved` option on an event-sourced aggregate now also covers the generated `add_<name>` and `remove_<name>` helpers of a removed association. During replay, a retained `@apply` handler that calls one of them on a reserved name no longer raises `AttributeError`: the call does nothing and returns `None`, and the child entities it would have added are not rebuilt. `get_one_from_<name>` and `filter_<name>` still raise, and so does any helper call on the live `raise_` path.
