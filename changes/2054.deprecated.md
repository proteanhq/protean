[CLI] Deprecate the marker names that `protean test test-adapter --capabilities` takes, such as `basic_storage`, and the `--test-dir` option. Both keep working for `--port database`, raise `RemovedInProtean020Warning`, and are removed in 0.20.0. Pass `DatabaseCapabilities` flag names, such as `CRUD`, instead of marker names, and omit `--test-dir`. (#2054)

**Migration:** [`test-adapter` marker names in `--capabilities` are deprecated](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#test-adapter-marker-names-in-capabilities-are-deprecated), [`test-adapter --test-dir` is deprecated](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#test-adapter-test-dir-is-deprecated)
