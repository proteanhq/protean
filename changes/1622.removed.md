**Breaking.** Removed the `protean.testing.assert_valid` and `protean.testing.assert_invalid` helpers, deprecated since 0.16.1. Use `pytest.raises(ValidationError, match=...)` in place of `assert_invalid`, and call the operation directly in place of `assert_valid`. The `protean._deprecation.RemovedInProtean018Warning` warning class is removed with them, since nothing is scheduled for removal in 0.18.0 any more. Delete any `-W` flag or `filterwarnings` entry that names it.

**Migration:** [`assert_valid` and `assert_invalid` are removed](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#assert_valid-and-assert_invalid-are-removed)
