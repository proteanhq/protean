Added machine-checked TLA+ specifications of the two protocols behind Protean's delivery guarantees, under `specs/`: the `$all` subscription checkpoint advance (the gap-safe low-watermark from #1088) and the transactional outbox two-phase publish. TLC checks each protocol's safety and liveness invariants exhaustively over every interleaving of out-of-order commits, lock expiry, redelivery, and crash points, and `specs/README.md` maps each invariant to the guarantee it verifies in `docs/reference/guarantees.md`. This is design-time verification, not a CI gate.
