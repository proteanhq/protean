A run-to-run stability metric for the comparison-eval harness under `tests/eval/` (kept out of the wheel): run a task `n` times for one approach and get two numbers back. The identical-result rate says how often the runs built the same structure, compared over the IR element-signature sets, alongside a count of the distinct structures. The score spread says how much the correctness score moved: mean, population standard deviation, min, and max. The deterministic approach runs in core CI as a zero-variance guard that re-scaffolds the gold; the context-driven variance number comes from the opt-in live lane.
