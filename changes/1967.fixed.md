**Breaking.** [IR] `deprecated=` raises `ConfigurationError` when `since` or `removal` is a number, and `protean ir diff` exits 1 on an IR file with a malformed `deprecated` block, such as a version written as a number or a missing `since`. A number lost its trailing zero, so `removal: 0.20` was read as `"0.2"`, and `diff_ir(current_version="0.18")` classed the field as safe to remove.

**Migration:** [Deprecation versions must be strings](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#deprecation-versions-must-be-strings)
