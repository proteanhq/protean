[Database] **Breaking.** A negated `Q` that combines conditions with `|` or `&` now excludes the records that match the whole expression on the memory, SQL and Elasticsearch providers. Before, `filter(~(Q(kind="dog") | Q(kind="fish")))` returned the dogs and fish it should have left out, and `~~Q(kind="dog")` returned every record except the dogs. `exclude()` given a positional `Q` now drops the records that match it: before, `exclude(Q(kind="dog"))` returned the dogs, the same records as `filter()`. Each positional `Q` and each keyword is negated on its own, so `exclude(Q(kind="dog"), Q(name="Rex"))` drops a record that matches either one. `~Q(a=1, b=2)` now means "not both", the same as `~(Q(a=1) & Q(b=2))`. `exclude(a=1, b=2)` keeps its meaning and drops a record that matches either condition. `~Q()` and `exclude(Q())` still filter nothing. (#2020)

**Migration:** [A negated `Q` excludes the whole expression](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#a-negated-q-excludes-the-whole-expression)
