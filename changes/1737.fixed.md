The add-validation skill's examples now store money as `Decimal(precision=19, scale=4)` instead of `Float`, so the order total check compares exactly. Before, an order with subtotals of 0.1 and 0.2 and a total of 0.3 failed its own invariant. `salary`, `Money.amount`, `Price.amount`, `total_amount`, `price` and `subtotal` changed; latitude and longitude stay `Float`.
