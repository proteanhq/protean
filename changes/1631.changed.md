**Breaking.** When a nested `UnitOfWork` fails and the outermost one then commits, `commit()` now rolls the transaction back and raises instead of logging a warning and returning. It raises the same exception that failed the nested `UnitOfWork`, or `TransactionError` when a nested `UnitOfWork` called `rollback()` with no exception. A handler or a `@use_case` that catches the error from a synchronous `domain.process(...)` and carries on now gets that error when its Unit of Work ends. An outer `@handle` handler retries the whole chain when its own retry policy covers the exception.

**Migration:** [An outer commit after a nested failure raises](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#an-outer-commit-after-a-nested-failure-raises)
