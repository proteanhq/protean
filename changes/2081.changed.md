The `DAO` service gains public `DAO.fetch`, `DAO.count` and `DAO.raw` methods, and `QuerySet` reads only through them. `DAO.fetch` and `DAO.raw` turn rows into entities, mark them retrieved and track aggregates in the Unit of Work; `DAO.fetch` returns `Record` objects when given `fields`. `DAO.raw` raises `NotSupportedError` when the provider does not declare `RAW_QUERIES`. The read path lives in `protean.infra.dao_read`, and the private `_filter`, `_count` and `_raw` forwarders are removed.
