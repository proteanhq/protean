**Breaking, custom repositories.** The `DAO` service gains public `DAO.fetch`, `DAO.count` and `DAO.raw` methods, and `QuerySet` reads only through them. `DAO.fetch` and `DAO.raw` turn rows into entities, mark them retrieved and, inside a Unit of Work, track aggregates in it; `DAO.fetch` returns `Record` objects when given `fields`. `DAO.raw` raises `NotSupportedError` when the provider does not declare `RAW_QUERIES`. The read path lives in `protean.infra.dao_read`, and the private `_filter`, `_count` and `_raw` forwarders are removed. A `QuerySet.raw()` that raises `NotSupportedError` now keeps the results the QuerySet had cached.

**Migration:** [`BaseDAO` is split into `DAOPort` and the `DAO` service](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#basedao-is-split-into-daoport-and-the-dao-service)
