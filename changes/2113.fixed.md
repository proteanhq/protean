`StreamSubscription` now keeps one retry count per stream and entry id. With priority lanes on, the primary and backfill streams assign Redis ids independently and can both hold an entry with the same id. Before, the two entries shared one count: a success on one stream cleared the other's count, so a failing entry could retry past `max_retries` and never reach the dead-letter queue. Each entry now reaches its own dead-letter queue after `max_retries` failures, and its `_dlq_metadata.retry_count` is that stream's count. The keys of `retry_counts` are now `"<stream>:<entry id>"`.
