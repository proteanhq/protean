A validator that raises `ValueError` on a `ValueObject` or `Reference` field is now reported under the field name, as on a `String` field: `{'email': ['rejected']}` in place of `{'_entity': ['rejected']}`. Assigning the field and `_update_data` now raise `ValidationError` where they raised the validator's bare `ValueError`. Every failing validator's message is reported, and construction keeps the `is required` error of a missing required `ValueObject` or `Reference` field next to a validator's error.
