Added the consumer side of `sequential_by` per-key sequential processing (ADR-0028), turning it on for event handlers and process managers on the Redis broker (which now advertises `STREAM_PARTITIONING`). A new `PartitionedStreamSubscription` reads every live partition stream of a category and drains each key strictly in order, with a single active consumer per partition across engine instances. The single-consumer guarantee is enforced by an ownership lease plus a fencing token (a per-partition generation), not a per-message lock: a slow or stalled owner can never let two different same-key events run at once or land out of order, and a stale owner that lost its lease is fenced out of both reading and acking. On owner crash another instance takes over at a new generation and reclaims the dead owner's pending entries (`XAUTOCLAIM`) so nothing is lost or skipped. A partition that cannot process its head message halts (no auto-DLQ, no advance past the poison) scoped to that one partition while others keep flowing. Partitions are discovered from a maintained per-category index the publisher writes (`{stream_category}:__partitions__`) — no keyspace scan — and drained cold partitions are reaped to keep the index bounded. Process managers own by correlation value spanning their subscribed categories. New broker port methods back this (partition index read/write, lease acquire/renew/release with a generation, fenced read/ack, `XAUTOCLAIM` reclaim, cold-partition reap), and the consumer timing is tunable under `[server.partitioning]` (`lease_ttl_ms`, `heartbeat_interval_seconds`, `poll_interval_seconds`, `reap_idle_ms`).
