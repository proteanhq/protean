`protean ir diff` now downgrades an event-sourced aggregate's breaking field changes (a field removal, a type change, or a required-field add) to safe when every rebuilding event that was version-bumped is covered by a registered upcaster, and at least one was bumped and covered. An event-sourced aggregate holds no schema of its own, so its field changes are earned-safe on the same terms as the events that replay it. A single uncovered bump among the rebuilding events, or no bump at all, leaves the aggregate breaking. A classic table-backed aggregate is never touched by this path; `exclude` stays the only escape hatch there.
