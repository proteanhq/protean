The inline and Redis PubSub brokers now handle a stream name that contains a colon, such as a partition stream. Before, the inline broker's `dlq_list`, `dlq_inspect`, `dlq_replay`, `dlq_replay_all` and `dlq_purge` did not find dead-lettered messages from such a stream, its `health()` reported a wrong consumer group name, and the Redis PubSub broker's `health()` left the stream out of `total_messages`.
