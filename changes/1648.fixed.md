Outbox reconcile now repairs only the events this domain owns. It no longer copies another domain's events, this domain's commands, process-manager transition events, or events of aggregates on another provider into the outbox being reconciled, and a rebuilt row keeps its `sequential_by` partition key. `protean outbox reconcile` now names both `default_subscription_type = "stream"` and `enable_outbox` when the outbox is off.
