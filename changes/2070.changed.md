**Breaking.** `read_blocking()` and `read_blocking_streams()` on a broker take `limit` in place of `count`, and return `BrokerMessage` records that unpack as `(id, payload)`. A broker adapter that declares `BrokerCapabilities.BLOCKING_READ` now subclasses the new `BlockingReadPort` Protocol from `protean.port.broker` and implements `read_blocking` and `read_blocking_streams` itself. The `_read_blocking` hook and the `_read_blocking_streams` default, which waited on each stream in turn, are removed. `domain.init()` raises `ConfigurationError` when a broker declares `BLOCKING_READ` and does not subclass `BlockingReadPort`. On a broker without `BLOCKING_READ`, the `Broker` service reads each stream once with `read` and does not wait, and no longer calls a `read_blocking` the adapter overrides.

**Migration:** [Broker `read_blocking()` and `read_blocking_streams()` take `limit`](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#broker-read_blocking-and-read_blocking_streams-take-limit)
