**Breaking.** [Domain] Remove the email port, deprecated since 0.17: `@domain.email`, `Domain.send_email()`, `Domain.get_email_provider()`, `BaseEmail`, `BaseEmailProvider`, the Dummy and SendGrid providers, the `sendgrid` extra, and the `DEPRECATED_EMAIL` and `DEPRECATED_CONFIG` check codes. An `[email_providers]` block in `domain.toml` is now ignored. (#1621)

**Migration:** [Email port removed](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#email-port-removed)
