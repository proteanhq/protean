**Breaking (driver).** The PostgreSQL provider defaults to psycopg 3. `protean[postgresql]` now installs `psycopg[binary]`, and the new `protean[postgresql-psycopg2]` extra installs `psycopg2-binary`. A `database_uri` that names its driver (`postgresql+psycopg://` or `postgresql+psycopg2://`) uses that driver. A plain `postgresql://` uses psycopg 3 when it is installed and psycopg2 otherwise, on SQLAlchemy 2.0 and 2.1 alike. A driver that cannot be imported fails at `domain.init()` with a `ConfigurationError` naming the extra to install. The SQL extras also accept SQLAlchemy 2.1 (`>=2.0.36,<2.2`).

**Migration:** [PostgreSQL defaults to psycopg 3](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#postgresql-defaults-to-psycopg-3)
