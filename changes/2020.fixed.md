A negated `Q` that combines conditions with `|` or `&` now excludes the records that match the whole expression on the memory, SQL and Elasticsearch providers. Before, `filter(~(Q(kind="dog") | Q(kind="fish")))` returned the dogs and fish it should have left out, and `exclude()` given such a `Q` kept the records it should have dropped. `~Q(a=1, b=2)` now means "not both", the same as `~(Q(a=1) & Q(b=2))`. `exclude(a=1, b=2)` keeps its meaning and drops a record that matches either condition.
