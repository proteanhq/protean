A QuerySet evaluated in one `UnitOfWork` transaction, or outside any, now runs its query again when it is read in a different transaction, by iterating it, indexing it, or reading `first`, `last` or `items`. Before, it handed out copies of its cached results. Inside a `UnitOfWork`, a copy of an aggregate the transaction tracked was a second instance of it: events raised on the copy were not saved, and saving the copy dropped the events pending on the tracked instance. The cached values could also be older than what was stored.
