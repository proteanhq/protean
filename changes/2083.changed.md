**Breaking.** `DAO.outside_uow()` returns a new DAO that works outside the active Unit of Work, and leaves the DAO it is called on unchanged. Before, it set a flag on that DAO, so every later operation through the repository's DAO ran outside the Unit of Work. The Unit of Work now flushes, commits and rolls back each session through its provider's `flush`, `commit` and `rollback`, and no longer calls `begin()` on a session. A custom database adapter defines `flush(session)` on its provider and returns a session with its transaction open from `get_session()`. Under `occ_trace`, an update through a Unit of Work on a SQL database records its outcome.

**Migration:** [`outside_uow()` returns a new DAO](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#outside_uow-returns-a-new-dao) and [The Unit of Work flushes and commits through the provider](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#the-unit-of-work-flushes-and-commits-through-the-provider)
