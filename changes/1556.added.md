Added the `split-aggregate` teaching skill to the DX pack. It walks through splitting an oversized aggregate along its consistency boundary. The worked example is an `Order` that has grown a second, fulfilment concern (packages, tracking events, delivery attempts) on top of the order concern (items, discounts, gift wraps): six child entities in one cluster, over the default `aggregate_size_limit`, so `check` reports `AGGREGATE_TOO_LARGE`. The after asset extracts the fulfilment concern into its own `Shipment` aggregate, links it back to `Order` by identity (an `Identifier` field holding the order's id, not a `Reference` that would trip `CROSS_AGGREGATE_REFERENCE`), and carries the cross-aggregate step with a domain event: `Order` raises `OrderPlaced`, an event handler in `Order`'s own cluster issues `OpenShipment`, and `Shipment`'s command handler opens the shipment. Both assets are runnable, and the after asset's whole hop runs in-process. The skill declares `AGGREGATE_TOO_LARGE` and `CROSS_AGGREGATE_REFERENCE`. (#1556)
