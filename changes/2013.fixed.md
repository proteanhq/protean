The OpenTelemetry guide and the observability reference now describe what the `/metrics` fallback endpoint emits. Before, the guide said the fallback behaved identically to the OpenTelemetry path, and the reference said the OpenTelemetry path returned its metrics instead of the listed gauges. The fallback serves the outbox, broker, subscription, pool and projection gauges plus the stream and per-consumer gauges, and none of the OpenTelemetry counters and histograms.
