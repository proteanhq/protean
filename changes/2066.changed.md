**Breaking.** [Cache] `BaseCache` is replaced by three protocols in `protean.port.cache`: `CachePort`, which every cache adapter subclasses, and `CacheTTLPort` and `CachePatternPort`, which an adapter also subclasses when it declares `TTL` or `PATTERN_MATCH`. Adapters store dicts under string keys. A new `Cache` class in `protean.infra.cache` builds keys, serializes projections, resolves TTLs and checks capabilities, and `domain.caches` and `domain.cache_for()` return it. `flush_all()` is renamed `clear()`, the adapter's `conn_info` is renamed `config`, and `Caches` moved to `protean.infra.cache`. `Domain.init()` raises `ConfigurationError` when an adapter declares a capability without subclassing its protocol. (#2066)
