A failed post-invariant now rolls back the change that broke it. The field keeps its previous value and the entity is not marked as changed. This covers every kind of field (plain, value object, `Reference`, `HasOne` and `HasMany`) and `add_<field>()`/`remove_<field>()` calls, on aggregates and child entities, whether assigned directly or inside an aggregate method. It also covers an invariant that fails with an error other than `ValidationError`. Earlier assignments in the same method that passed their checks stay applied. When an `atomic_change` block raises or its exit check fails, every change made in the block is undone, including child entities and associations. On an event-sourced aggregate, a failed `raise_()` undoes the field changes its `@apply` handler made. A child rejected by `HasOne` assignment or `add_<field>()` is left unlinked from the aggregate.
