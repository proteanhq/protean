A failed post-invariant now rolls back the assignment that broke it, as the invariants guide documents. The field keeps its previous value and the entity is not marked as changed. This covers plain fields, value-object fields and `Reference` fields, on aggregates and child entities, whether assigned directly or inside an aggregate method. Earlier assignments in the same method that passed their checks stay applied, and `atomic_change` still leaves its batch applied when its exit check fails.
