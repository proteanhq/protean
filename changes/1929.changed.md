**Breaking.** A `SIGTERM` no longer fails the rest of a batch the engine has already read: the batch keeps handling its messages until `server.drain_timeout` ends. Messages a stopped worker read and did not handle stay pending in Redis, and a starting subscription now claims the ones that have waited `server.reclaim_idle_seconds` (120 by default) and handles them, counting their earlier deliveries toward `max_retries`. `POST /drainz` now reports `"drained": true` once the worker has no batch in flight.

**Migration:** [A shutdown finishes the batch in hand, and a start claims what stopped workers left](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#a-shutdown-finishes-the-batch-in-hand-and-a-start-claims-what-stopped-workers-left)
