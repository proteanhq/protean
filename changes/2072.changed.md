**Breaking.** A broker adapter that declares `BrokerCapabilities.STREAM_PARTITIONING` now subclasses the new `PartitioningPort` Protocol from `protean.port.broker` and implements its nine partition methods, such as `record_partition` and `read_partition_fenced`, itself. The underscore hooks on `BaseBroker` and the public wrappers that called them are removed. `read_partition_fenced` and `reclaim_partition_pending` take `limit` in place of `count` and return `BrokerMessage` records, and `ack_partition_fenced` takes `message_id` in place of `identifier`. Stream trimming has its own flag, `BrokerCapabilities.STREAM_RETENTION`, and its own Protocol, `StreamRetentionPort`, which declares `trim(stream)`. The Redis broker declares the flag, and `ENTERPRISE_STREAMING` includes it. `BaseBroker.trim` and `InlineBroker.trim` are removed, and on a broker without `STREAM_RETENTION` the `Broker` service skips trimming. `domain.init()` raises `ConfigurationError` when a broker declares either flag and does not subclass its Protocol. The `Broker` service has no public partition methods or `trim`.

**Migration:** [Broker partition methods move to `PartitioningPort`](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#broker-partition-methods-move-to-partitioningport) and [Stream trimming has its own `STREAM_RETENTION` flag](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#stream-trimming-has-its-own-stream_retention-flag)
