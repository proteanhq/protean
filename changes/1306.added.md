Documented the [broker partitioning contract](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/adapters/broker/partitioning/): the partition-discovery, fenced-ownership and stream-trimming methods an adapter implements to support `sequential_by`, and the `LeaseLostError` they raise. It was a public port surface with no documentation at all, so `sequential_by` was documented as a feature without its contract. Also fills gaps in the existing references: `[server.partitioning]` tunables and `[lint].check_adapter_calls` now appear in the configuration reference, `protean server` documents its `--acknowledge-event-store-risk` alias, `protean upgrade-check` lists its three new Unit-of-Work findings and warns that they widen exit code 2, and the custom-profile allowed-field list no longer omits the two circuit-breaker keys.
