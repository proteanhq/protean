The multi-worker Supervisor now sizes its shutdown budget from `server.drain_timeout`. It waits the drain window plus 5 seconds before it kills a worker, and never less than 30 seconds, so a worker with a drain window above 25 seconds is no longer killed mid-drain. The Supervisor and `protean server --reload` also find the project config for a dotted `--domain` such as `src.shop.domain:domain`, the form the scaffolded Dockerfile uses. The engine no longer logs a startup warning when `drain_timeout` reaches 30 seconds.
