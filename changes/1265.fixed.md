Reconcile the subscription and InlineBroker retry/DLQ layers so a subscription owns retry and dead-lettering end-to-end. For a stream consumed by a `BrokerSubscription` or a `StreamSubscription` (the default type), the InlineBroker now holds and redelivers a nacked message with no independent ceiling of its own: a persistent `{stream}:dlq` outage no longer routes the message to the broker's native DLQ, its stale-in-flight timeout sweep holds the message instead of dead-lettering it, and no `retry_counts` entry leaks. The subscription's `{stream}:dlq` is the single dead-letter destination for these streams. The InlineBroker also caps its exponential-backoff exponent and delay so an unbounded owned-stream retry count cannot overflow and freeze a message.
