**Breaking.** Reconcile the subscription and InlineBroker retry/DLQ layers so a subscription owns retry and dead-lettering end-to-end. For a stream consumed by a `BrokerSubscription` or a `StreamSubscription` (the default type), the InlineBroker now holds and redelivers a nacked message with no independent ceiling of its own: a persistent `{stream}:dlq` outage no longer routes the message to the broker's native DLQ, its stale-in-flight timeout sweep holds the message instead of dead-lettering it, and no `retry_counts` entry leaks. The subscription's `{stream}:dlq` is the single dead-letter destination for these streams, so `protean dlq list` no longer surfaces such a message out of the broker's native DLQ. The InlineBroker also caps its backoff delay so neither an unbounded owned-stream retry count nor a large configured `backoff_multiplier` can overflow the calculation and freeze a message.

**Migration:** [The InlineBroker no longer dead-letters a message its subscription owns](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#the-inlinebroker-no-longer-dead-letters-a-message-its-subscription-owns)
