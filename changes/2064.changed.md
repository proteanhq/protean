`protean test` runs the event-store conformance suite on Message DB in the `message-db` leg, in `-c FULL`, in `-c COVERAGE` and in `-c EVENTSTORE`. A new `conformance` key on an entry in `tests/adapters.toml` lists the `"<port>:<adapter>"` pairs that the entry's leg, `-c FULL` and `-c COVERAGE` run. `-c EVENTSTORE` runs the suite on each event store in its own store list except memory.
