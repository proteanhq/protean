The Redis broker's `read_blocking` now returns at once when `timeout_ms` is `0`. Before, it sent `BLOCK 0` to Redis, which means "wait forever". The call then hung until the client's socket timed out and logged `broker.redis.read_blocking_failed`. This hit every priority-lane subscription on each tick, because its primary-stream read uses a zero timeout. A `blocking_timeout_ms` of `0` now polls without waiting.
