Event-store subscriptions now report their exhausted positions as `dlq_depth` in `protean subscriptions status`, `/readyz`, the observatory and the `protean_subscription_dlq_depth` gauge. The count is the positions whose latest record in the subscription's `failed-*` stream is `Exhausted`, the same positions `protean eventstore dlq list` shows; it used to be `0`. `protean eventstore dlq inspect` no longer shows a position that was replayed or purged after it was exhausted.
