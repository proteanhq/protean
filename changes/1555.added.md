Added the `add-saga-flow` teaching skill to the DX pack. It sequences the `process-manager` element skill into an end-to-end saga: a broker-free order-fulfillment flow across Order, Inventory, Payment, and Shipping, coordinated by one process manager that reacts to each step's event, issues the next command, and closes on a terminal `end=True` handler with a compensating failure path. It ships two runnable assets, one that leaves the saga open and one that closes it, and declares `PROCESS_MANAGER_UNCLOSED`, the diagnostic `check` reports for a process manager with no `end=True` handler.
