[Fields] **Breaking.** Field validator errors have one shape on every field kind. `ValidationError.messages` is a plain `dict[str, list[str]]` for simple fields and for `ValueObject`, `ValueObjectList` and `Reference` fields. A validator that raises a list has its messages listed under the field name, and a validator that raises a dict has its keys joined to the field name with a dot, so `{"code": ["has x"]}` on field `b` gives `{'b.code': ['has x']}`. Codes a validator raises appear in `e.codes` on aggregates, entities, value objects, commands, events, queries and projections. Constructing an aggregate or an entity reports every failing simple, `ValueObject`, `ValueObjectList` and `Reference` field. (#1954)

**Migration:** [Field validator errors carry a plain `dict` with dotted keys](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#field-validator-errors-carry-a-plain-dict-with-dotted-keys)
