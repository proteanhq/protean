Fixed three gaps in subscription status reporting, which feeds `protean subscriptions status`, the `protean.subscription.*` metrics, the Observatory and the `/readyz` probe. Outbox processors configured through `outbox.external_brokers` now get a row instead of being counted but never described; providers with `managed = false` no longer get a row for a processor the engine never started; and a `sequential_by` category now sums lag across its per-key partition streams instead of reading an empty base stream and reporting `unknown`. A lag that cannot be computed is also reported as `null` rather than falling back to the pending count, which made an unreadable subscription look healthy.
