**Breaking, custom database adapters.** `DAO.claim` now adds one to the `_version` of each aggregate row it claims, and the returned entity carries the new value. A custom adapter that declares `NATIVE_CLAIM` must do the same in its `claim`. The portable claim and the PostgreSQL claim already do. (#1964)

**Migration:** [A claim advances the row's version](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#a-claim-advances-the-rows-version)
