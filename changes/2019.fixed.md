**Breaking.** `del domain.caches[name]` and `del domain.providers[name]` raise `KeyError` naming the key when `name` is not present, before and after `domain.init()`. Before `domain.init()` they raised a bare `AssertionError`, and after it a missing name was ignored.

**Migration:** [Deleting an absent cache or provider raises `KeyError`](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#deleting-an-absent-cache-or-provider-raises-keyerror)
