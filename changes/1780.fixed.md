`domain.to_ir()` no longer fails with a `TypeError` when a field declares a `decimal.Decimal` `min_value` or `max_value`. A `decimal.Decimal` bound on a field whose IR type is `Decimal` now goes into the IR as a decimal string (`"0.01"`). An `int` or `float` bound stays a number. The v0.3.0 IR schema now accepts a number or a string for `min_value` and `max_value`.
