`domain.to_ir()` no longer fails with a `TypeError` when a field declares a `decimal.Decimal` `min_value` or `max_value`. A `decimal.Decimal` bound on a field whose IR type is `Decimal` now goes into the IR as a decimal string (`"0.01"`). An `int` or `float` bound stays a number. An `Integer` or `Float` field always writes its bounds as numbers, so `Integer(min_value="5")` now writes `5`. The v0.3.0 IR schema accepts a number or a string for `min_value` and `max_value`, and still requires a number on an `Integer` or `Float` field.
