Multi-worker (`--workers N`) and `--reload` workers of `protean server` now log at `PROTEAN_LOG_LEVEL` from the moment they start, as the parent process does. Before, a worker logged at INFO until its domain loaded, so `PROTEAN_LOG_LEVEL=DEBUG` lost the DEBUG lines written while the domain loaded. `--log-level` still takes precedence, and `--log-config` still replaces both.
