`protean ir diff` now reports the changes that stop an event-sourced aggregate being rebuilt from its events. Turning event sourcing on or off, moving an aggregate's `stream_category`, pointing its identity at another field, and dropping an `@apply` handler are each breaking on their own, whether or not the aggregate's fields moved. An event-sourced aggregate stores no schema of its own, so these were previously invisible: a domain could move a stream category, strand every existing aggregate's history, and still pass the compatibility check clean.
