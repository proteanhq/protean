**Breaking.** `protean ir diff` now reports the changes that stop an event-sourced aggregate being rebuilt from its events. Turning event sourcing on or off, moving an aggregate's `stream_category`, pointing its identity at another field, and dropping an `@apply` handler while its event survives are each breaking on their own, whether or not the aggregate's fields moved. An event-sourced aggregate's authoritative state is a stream of events, so none of these is a field change and none was reported anywhere: a domain could move a stream category, strand every existing aggregate's history, and still pass the compatibility check clean. A domain that made one of these changes and passed before will now fail the check.

**Migration:** [Replay hazards on an event-sourced aggregate are now breaking](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#replay-hazards-on-an-event-sourced-aggregate-are-now-breaking)
