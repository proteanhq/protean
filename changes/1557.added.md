Added the `extract-bounded-context` teaching skill to the DX pack. It shows how to take a set of aggregates out of one `Domain` (bounded context) into a second `Domain` and wire the two by domain events across the seam: the owning context publishes an event as its published language, and the other consumes it through a subscriber and an anti-corruption layer, holding the far side by identity. The before/after example targets `CIRCULAR_CLUSTER_DEPENDENCY`. The before is one domain whose sales and fulfilment clusters reference each other in a cycle; the after splits them into two domains that talk by events, clearing the cycle and the `CROSS_AGGREGATE_REFERENCE` co-smell.
