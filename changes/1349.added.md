Scoring for the comparison-eval harness under `tests/eval/` (kept out of the wheel): a per-task `spec.json` recipe, a deterministic gold built from `protean new` + `protean add`, and a per-element rubric that scores a produced project's IR against the gold by class name. `compare()` runs both the deterministic and the context-driven approach over a task and reports each one's `protean verify` verdict plus its correctness score.
