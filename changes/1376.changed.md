**Breaking.** `cffi` and `greenlet` are no longer core dependencies. Nothing in Protean imports either package; they were unpinned transitive dependencies of the SQLAlchemy adapter extras and of `cryptography` (via the `sendgrid` extra). Their floors moved to the extras that pull them (`greenlet>=3.2.3,<4` on `postgresql`/`sqlite`/`mssql`, `cffi>=2.0.0` on `sendgrid`), so installing one of those extras still resolves a version with wheels for the newest supported Python. If your own code imports `cffi` or `greenlet`, declare it in your project.

**Migration:** [cffi and greenlet left the core install](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-18/#cffi-and-greenlet-left-the-core-install)
