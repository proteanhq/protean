Broker subscribers can opt into consume-side idempotency with `@domain.subscriber(idempotent=True)`. Protean records a marker for each payload's `metadata.headers.id` in the subscriber's `provider` (default `"default"`), in the same unit of work as the subscriber's writes, and skips a redelivered payload. An idempotent subscriber treats a payload with no `metadata.headers.id` as an error, and `domain.init()` rejects an idempotent subscriber whose provider is not configured or not managed (#1932)
