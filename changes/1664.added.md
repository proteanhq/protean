The IR now records the save-time hooks it used to leave out. A `Date` or `DateTime` field declared with `auto_now=True` or `auto_now_add=True` carries `"auto_now": true` or `"auto_now_add": true`, and the `domain` section lists the registered aggregate, command and event enrichers by qualified name, in registration order. Both are left out when unused, so the IR and checksum of a domain that uses neither do not change. `protean ir diff` now reports adding, removing or changing either one. (#1664)
