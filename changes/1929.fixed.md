A `SIGTERM` no longer fails the rest of a batch the engine has already read. The batch keeps handling its messages until `server.drain_timeout` ends. Messages a stopped worker read and did not handle stay pending in Redis, and a starting subscription now claims the ones that have waited `server.reclaim_idle_seconds` (120 by default) and handles them, counting their earlier deliveries toward `max_retries`. `POST /drainz` now reports `"drained": true` once the worker has no batch in flight.
