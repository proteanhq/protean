**Breaking.** `protean eventstore verify` now exits 2 when it cannot connect to the event store. Under `--json` it prints one error envelope with the connection error under `data.error`; without `--json` it prints the error as one line on stderr. Before, it exited 1 with a logged traceback and, under `--json`, no envelope.

**Migration:** [`eventstore verify` exits 2 when it cannot connect](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#eventstore-verify-exits-2-when-it-cannot-connect)
