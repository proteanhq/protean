Added a `lag_drain_rate` to every per-subscription row in the `/readyz` `checks.subscriptions.details` block. It is the change in `lag_seconds` per second: negative while a backlog drains, positive while it grows. The health server's refresher keeps a bounded sliding window of the last 30 lag samples per subscription and fits a least-squares slope over them. It reads `null` until two refreshes have landed, and for any subscription whose `lag_seconds` is unknown. An unreadable lag also starts that subscription's window over, so the first refresh after it becomes readable again reads `null` rather than a slope stretched across the gap. It is not a `SubscriptionStatus` field and not a new metrics gauge: a metrics backend derives the same signal from the existing lag-seconds gauge.
