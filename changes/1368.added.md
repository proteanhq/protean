Added a `lag_drain_rate` to every per-subscription row in the `/readyz` `checks.subscriptions.details` block. It is the change in `lag_seconds` per second, computed by least-squares over a bounded sliding window the health-server refresher keeps: negative while a backlog drains, positive while it grows. It reads `null` until two refreshes have landed and for any subscription whose `lag_seconds` is unknown. It is not a `SubscriptionStatus` field and not a new metrics gauge; a metrics backend derives the same signal from the existing lag-seconds gauge.
