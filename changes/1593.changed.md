The MySQL and MSSQL providers now check their default driver at `domain.init()`. When a `database_uri` names PyMySQL or pyodbc, or names no driver, and that driver cannot be imported, `domain.init()` raises a `ConfigurationError` that names the database, the driver and the extra to install (`protean[mysql]` or `protean[mssql]`). It used to raise the driver's own `ModuleNotFoundError` from SQLAlchemy. A `database_uri` that names another driver goes to SQLAlchemy as written.
