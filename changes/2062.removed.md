**Breaking.** [Event store] Remove `BaseEventStore` from `protean.port.event_store`. `MemoryEventStore` and `MessageDBStore` subclass `EventStorePort`, and a custom event store subclasses `EventStorePort` and sets `name`, `domain` and `config` in its own `__init__`. The adapter no longer has `read`, `read_all`, `read_last_message`, `append`, `load_aggregate`, `create_snapshot`, `create_snapshots`, `category`, `stream_head_position`, `trace_causation`, `trace_effects`, `build_causation_tree` or `verify`; call them on `domain.event_store`, and call `category_head_position` in place of `stream_head_position`. The `store` property of the `EventStore` service is removed as well; call the service methods on `domain.event_store`. (#2062)

**Migration:** [`BaseEventStore` is removed; event store adapters implement `EventStorePort`](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#baseeventstore-is-removed-event-store-adapters-implement-eventstoreport)
