The comparison-eval harness under `tests/eval/` now keys transcripts to the pack minor (`transcripts/0.19.0/` serves every 0.19.x release), so a patch release replays its minor's transcript and records nothing. The replay lane accepts only a live recording: a transcript needs `"recorded_by": "live"` and the `model_id` of the model that ran, which the live lane stamps from a new `model_id` attribute on the live driver. A new minor with no transcript of its own fails the guard instead of replaying an older minor's files. The earlier `place_order` transcripts carried no live mark and are removed.
