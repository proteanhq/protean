**Breaking.** [Broker] Remove `BaseBroker` from `protean.port.broker`. `InlineBroker`, `RedisBroker` and `RedisPubSubBroker` subclass `BrokerPort` or a capability Port, and a custom broker subclasses `BrokerPort` and sets `name`, `domain` and `config` in its own `__init__`. The `Broker` service no longer has `has_capability`, `has_all_capabilities` or `has_any_capability`; check `broker.capabilities` with `in` and `&`. `OperationState` moves to `protean.adapters.broker.inline`, and the module-level retry and timeout defaults leave `protean.port.broker`. (#2073)

**Migration:** [`BaseBroker` is removed; broker adapters implement `BrokerPort`](https://docs.proteanhq.com/reference/migration/v0-19/#basebroker-is-removed-broker-adapters-implement-brokerport)
