An event-store subscription's recovery rebuild now reconciles a recovery-checkpoint watermark that a restore left ahead of the restored `failed-positions` stream. Before, reading forward from the stale watermark found nothing and left it in place, so a position that started failing after the restore (appended at a lower per-stream position) was skipped on every restart and never retried. The rebuild now discards the stale snapshot and rebuilds the set from the start of the stream when the watermark sits past the tail, and `protean recover --reset-beyond-head` persists the reconciled watermark.
