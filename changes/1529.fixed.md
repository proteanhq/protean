An event-store subscription's recovery rebuild now reconciles a recovery-checkpoint watermark that a restore left ahead of the restored `failed-positions` stream. Before, reading forward from the stale watermark found nothing and left it in place, so a position that started failing after the restore (appended at a lower per-stream position) was skipped on every restart and never retried. The rebuild now restarts the merge from the start of the stream when the watermark sits past the tail, so the watermark reconciles to the real tail and `protean recover --reset-beyond-head` persists the reconciled value. The checkpoint snapshot is kept, so a still-unresolved position it remembers whose failed record the restore rolled back is not lost: `protean recover` still reports or clears a stale one, and the runtime still retries a healthy one.
